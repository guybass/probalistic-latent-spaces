# Comparison note: PGA-01

The independently computed Fisher metrics agree with 9/800 and 81/5000 to 3.5e-18, while the dense-grid supremum KL fit has slope -1.9979 and n^2 max-KL remains bounded near 2.46e-4. This supports the paper claim that KL converges quadratically even though the metric at x=0 has a fixed nonzero gap; the grid is supporting evidence rather than a replacement for the analytic supremum argument.
